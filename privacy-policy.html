Privacy Policy - Tugi Care

Last updated: April 2026

Overview

Tugi Care Inc. ("Tugi Care", "Laine", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our applications, websites, and related services.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our services, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information such as your name and email address
  • User-generated content including notes, reminders, uploaded files, and documents
  • Conversation history and interactions with AI-powered features
  • Audio recordings or voice input when voice features are used
  • Health, wellness, smartwatch, and fitness-related information when permission is granted
  • Location information when location-based features are enabled
  • Device information, diagnostics, crash reports, and usage analytics

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and maintain our AI companion and caregiving services
  • Enable personalized features and wellness insights
  • Support caregiver and care recipient coordination
  • Improve app performance, reliability, and functionality
  • Respond to support requests and communicate with users
  • Protect security, prevent fraud, and enforce our policies

Health and Wellness Information

Some features may use health, wellness, fitness, or smartwatch-related data when you explicitly grant permission. This information is used solely to provide app functionality and wellness-related insights.

Laine is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or professional medical advice. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals regarding medical concerns.

AI and Third-Party Services

Some features may use third-party service providers, including AI and cloud infrastructure providers, to process information necessary for app functionality.

These providers process data on our behalf subject to contractual and security obligations.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

We may share information:

  • With service providers that help operate our services
  • When required by law or legal process
  • To protect users, rights, safety, or security
  • With your consent or at your direction

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Data Protection

  • Secure encrypted communication using industry-standard technologies
  • Security measures designed to protect against unauthorized access or disclosure
  • Access controls and limited internal access to sensitive information
  • Monitoring and technical safeguards to improve platform security and reliability

Your Rights

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and associated data
  • Withdraw permissions such as location or health access
  • Opt out of certain communications

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International Data Transfers

Information may be processed and stored in countries where we or our service providers operate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted with a revised effective date.
