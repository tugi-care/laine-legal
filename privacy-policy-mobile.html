Mobile App Privacy Policy - Laine

Last updated: May 19, 2026

App: Laine (iOS & Android)

Overview

This Mobile App Privacy Policy explains how Laine collects, uses, and protects information specifically related to the use of the Laine mobile application and its mobile device features.

Microphone and Audio Features

Laine may request microphone access to enable features such as:

  • Voice conversations with the AI companion
  • Speech-to-text input and voice commands
  • Audio-based interactions and accessibility features

Audio Data Processing

  • Audio may be processed in real time to support speech recognition and AI functionality
  • Processed transcripts may be used to generate AI responses and improve app functionality
  • Some audio or transcript data may be temporarily processed by trusted third-party AI or cloud service providers
  • We do not sell audio recordings or personal audio data to third parties

Health, Wellness, and Smartwatch Data

With your permission, Laine may access health, fitness, wellness, or smartwatch-related information to provide wellness insights, caregiving support, and personalized experiences.

Laine is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, or professional medical advice. Always consult qualified healthcare professionals regarding medical concerns.

Location Information

If enabled, Laine may collect location information to support safety, caregiving, coordination, or location-based features.

You can disable location access at any time in your device settings.

Other Permissions

  • Notifications: Used for reminders, alerts, and important updates
  • Network Access: Required for AI services, syncing, and cloud functionality
  • Storage and Files: Used for uploaded documents, app data, and preferences
  • Health and Fitness Permissions: Used for smartwatch and wellness-related features when enabled

Analytics and Diagnostics

We may collect diagnostics, crash reports, and usage analytics to improve app performance, reliability, security, and user experience.

Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal information to third parties
  • Information may be shared with trusted service providers that help operate app functionality
  • Information may be disclosed when required by law or to protect users and platform security

User Controls

  • Permissions such as microphone, health, fitness, notifications, and location access can be managed through device settings
  • Users may delete conversation history and uploaded content within the app where supported
  • Users may request deletion of their account and associated data

Children's Privacy

Laine is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Mobile App Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted with a revised effective date.
